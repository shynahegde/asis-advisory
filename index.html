Why Asis Exists

The AI advisory market is broken in a specific way.

Enterprises are spending more on AI advisory than ever. Most of it doesn't ship. Here's the pattern.

Most AI engagements produce strategy decks or isolated pilots that never reach production. The handoff from senior talent to junior delivery breaks the chain every time.

Enterprises are increasingly pulling AI work in-house because external advisors show up with theory and no delivery capability — then hand over a deck and rotate off.

Big-firm engagements are open-ended and slow. Boutique development shops are cheap and fast but lack the governance depth regulated industries require — and the credibility to operate at board level.

The market is bifurcated between "advise you" and "build for you." Almost no firm commits both capital and delivery talent to its own roadmap. That gap is where Asis operates.

Common Questions

Addressed directly.

How is Asis different from a Big Four or McKinsey engagement?
Three things. First, we're fixed-fee — no open-ended retainers or scope creep. Second, the senior operators who write your strategy are the same people who help execute it. There's no handoff to a junior team after the deck is signed. Third, we're sized for speed: 30-day first deliverable, 90-day execution engagement. Big-firm timelines run 12–24 months and are designed around their staffing model, not your urgency. We work at the pace regulated enterprises actually need to move to stay ahead of the regulatory calendar.
What if the readout says AI isn't the right fit for us yet?
We tell you. That's the point of the readout. If the honest answer is "your data infrastructure isn't ready," or "you have three foundational problems to solve before AI will stick," we say that — and we document it clearly, with a prioritized plan for what to fix first. We'd rather spend 30 days telling you the truth than 90 days executing the wrong thing. Clients who hear hard news from us at readout consistently come back when they're ready, because they trust the assessment.
How do you handle data security and IP during an engagement?
We operate under mutual NDA and a clearly scoped data handling agreement from the start of every engagement. For healthcare clients, we operate under BAA as required by HIPAA. We don't use client data to train models, we don't retain sensitive materials after engagement close, and all work product created during the engagement belongs to the client. For highly sensitive environments, we also support air-gapped or on-premises working arrangements — we've done it in clinical settings before.
We already have AI vendors and tools in place. Do we still start with a Diagnostic Sprint?
Yes — and the Diagnostic Sprint is often most valuable for organizations that already have AI in motion. The assessment tells you what's actually delivering value vs. what's consuming budget, identifies governance gaps created by rapid tool adoption, and gives you a prioritized view of what to scale, what to kill, and what to govern more tightly. If you're already running AI programs, the AI Health Check engagement may also be a fit — it's designed specifically for organizations that need an independent audit of an existing portfolio.
What happens after the 90-day engagement ends?
Your team owns everything we built. The governance framework, the AI intake process, the vendor vetting scorecard, the roadmap — all of it transfers to your organization with documentation designed for internal operation. Most clients continue with a quarterly AI Health Check to keep the roadmap current and the governance framework calibrated as regulations and the market shift. Some engage us for a second 90-day sprint on the next priority. Either way, the decision is yours — we've designed the engagement to leave you self-sufficient, not dependent.
